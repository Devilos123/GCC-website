GreenheraldComputer Club

Welcome to the official website of the Greenherald Computer Club. A student-run club for people who'd rather ship something broken than talk about something perfect. Weekly build nights, guest talks, and a Discord that never sleeps.

What we actually do

03 things

Build nights

Every Thursday. Bring a laptop and an idea, or don't — pair up with someone who has one.

Talks that aren't recruiting pitches

Alumni, professors, and the occasional stranger from the internet talk about what they're actually working on.

A CTF team that competes

We enter regional and online competitions. No experience required to start showing up to practice.
